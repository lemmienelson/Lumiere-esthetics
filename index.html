Meet Rosmery Guzman

Rosmery is the founder and owner of Lumière Esthetics, a private beauty studio in the heart of Cartagena. A certified esthetician specializing in lash extensions, lash lifting, brow lamination and ombré powder brows, she brings precision, warmth, and an eye for detail to every appointment.

Committed to continuous growth, Rosmery regularly attends advanced trainings to stay at the forefront of the latest techniques — ensuring every client receives the highest quality service and the longest-lasting results possible.

She is passionate about connecting with her clients and helping them feel beautiful inside and out.

— Rosmery Guzman
